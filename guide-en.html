Manuscript Photos

  1. Enter your email and tap Save and connect Google Drive. Choose your Google account and approve access. Only after this succeeds does the manuscript form appear.
  2. Enter the library and shelfmark.
  3. Open-book photo is selected initially. If your first photo is a single page, uncheck it, enter the folio and choose its side.
  4. Take the photo. Afterwards, add any notes and choose how to continue.
  5. Finish returns to a new manuscript. Keep the app open until Drive confirms saving.

The ☰ menu contains previous photos, profile changes, reconnecting Drive, opening the Drive folder and Log out / connect another account. Logout keeps local originals; they are not uploaded to a different account.

The language selector switches between Hebrew and English. Your saved photos and current details are kept.

Photos save under כתבי יד → Library_ddmmyyyy. Names include library, shelfmark, folio and side, with _001, _002 for repeated photos. The app saves JPG photos, plus a TXT file only when notes are entered.

If the connection stops, pending photos remain on this phone. Reconnect the same account through the menu. Do not clear website data before confirming your backup.

Photograph subject includes manuscript folios, numbered front or back flyleaves, front cover, spine and back cover. For an opening from the last front flyleaf to folio 1a, choose Flyleaf, enter its number, keep Open-book photo checked, choose Manuscript folio as the next page and enter 1. Binding photos do not require a folio.

Watermarks

Select Watermark photo before photographing with backlighting. Then use Photograph the same page without backlighting. Keep the page, camera position, angle and framing unchanged. Both images save in Library_date / Shelfmark / Watermarks, with linked lighting metadata. This prepares images for later analysis; it does not enhance them.
