Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

What the app does

Manuscript Photos is a free web app for recording manuscript photographs and their descriptions. It is an independent project, not affiliated with Google, GitHub, or the National Library of Israel.

Information and storage

The app stores your selected Google account email, preferences, manuscript library and shelfmark, folio and side, photographs, optional notes and watermark markings, and upload status in your browser on your device. Photographs may retain metadata embedded by your camera, including location if your camera records it. The app does not separately request your location.

After you authorize Google Drive, photographs and relevant descriptions are uploaded directly from your device to your own Drive, under כתבי יד. The photo-only edition uploads JPG photographs and a text file only when notes are entered. The personal edition also uploads descriptive metadata. Uploads are not sent to the app maintainer or stored in its GitHub repository. No dashboard connection or sharing with other app users is automatic.

Google authorization

Google handles sign-in. The app does not receive your Google password. It requests the drive.file permission for files created or explicitly used with the app, rather than general access to your whole Drive. Temporary access tokens are held in memory while the app is open. Uploaded files are used only to provide the requested capture and backup features. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Service providers

GitHub Pages serves the app and may process connection information such as IP addresses under its own policy. Google processes sign-in and Drive data under its policies. The app has no added advertising or analytics trackers. These providers have their own retention and security practices: GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect or change accounts in the app menu and revoke authorization in your Google account's third-party connections settings. Signing out does not erase local photographs or existing Drive uploads. Delete uploads in Google Drive and clear this site's browser storage to remove local data; first ensure any pending photographs are safely backed up. Browser storage may also be erased by your browser or operating system. Copies you share or download must be managed separately.

Security and contact

The site uses HTTPS, but no storage or transfer method is guaranteed secure. Protect your phone and Google account, keep backups, and check successful uploads before deleting originals. If you email support, the address and information you send will be used to respond and retained only as reasonably needed for that correspondence. Do not send sensitive photographs unnecessarily.

Changes

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Material changes to data use will be explained before they apply.
